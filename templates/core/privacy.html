What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, optional phone number, campus, and a securely hashed password.
  • Content you post: listings, photos, gigs, deals, messages, reviews and reports. Photo location data (EXIF/GPS) is removed when you upload.
  • Basic technical data: sign-in sessions for security, and when you were last active.

How we use it

  • To run CampusHub: show your listings, deliver messages and notifications, and process deal claims.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, prevent fraud and enforce our terms.
  • To understand usage in aggregate. We don't sell personal data.

Who can see what

  • Public: your name, campus, bio, active listings and reviews.
  • Private: your email and phone number. They are never shown on listings or profiles.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, and to moderators when a message is reported.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in Settings. We remove your name, email, phone and bio, take down your listings and keep anonymised transaction and moderation records needed for safety and legal obligations.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by emailing privacy@campushub.ug.
