Buying and selling

  • Meet in busy, public places on campus during the day: libraries, faculty receptions, the main gate.
  • Inspect the item before paying. For phones and laptops, power them on and check they aren't locked to another account.
  • Don't send mobile money in advance to someone you haven't met. Scammers often ask for a “transport fee” or “deposit”.
  • Keep conversations in CampusHub messages so there's a record if something goes wrong.
  • If a price looks too good to be true, it probably is. Ask for proof of ownership for expensive items.

Gigs and tasks

  • Agree on the scope, price and deadline in messages before starting.
  • For bigger jobs, agree to pay part on a first draft and the rest on completion.
  • Both people confirm completion in CampusHub. If something goes wrong, open a dispute on the gig page.
  • CampusHub doesn't allow gigs to sit exams or complete graded coursework for someone else. Report any you see.

Jobs and deals

  • Never pay a fee to apply for a job or to “unlock” an opportunity.
  • Deal codes are free to claim. Nobody should ask you to pay for a code.

Your account

  • CampusHub staff will never ask for your password or mobile money PIN.
  • Your phone number is private and isn't shown on your profile or listings.

Reporting

Use the Report button on any listing, gig, deal, business or profile. Reports are confidential and reviewed by our moderators, who can remove content and suspend accounts. For emergencies, contact the Uganda Police on 999 or 112, or your campus security office.
